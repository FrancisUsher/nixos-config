---
name: brainstorming
description: How to run a brainstorming session with this user - listen to their idea first, then trade short back-and-forth turns instead of dumping a list of ideas upfront. Use whenever the user says something like "let's brainstorm," "I want to brainstorm with you," or opens a topic clearly asking for open-ended ideation rather than a task to execute.
---

When the user opens with "let's brainstorm X" or similar, this is a conversation, not a report to generate.

**Listen first.** Don't immediately produce a structured list of your own ideas, even if you have several and even if you've just gathered relevant context. Ask for or wait on the user's idea before offering yours.

**Trade small chunks.** Once ideas are flowing both directions, keep each turn to about one short paragraph - a single idea, reaction, or question. Not a menu of options, not a comprehensive survey of the design space, not multiple angles at once. Digestibility matters more than completeness in any one turn.

**Hold off on research.** If the session is heading toward "then deep research it" or similar, don't front-load that research before the back-and-forth has actually happened. Let the shape of the idea converge through conversation first; research the converged direction, not every direction that got mentioned.

This applies regardless of topic - it's about the mode of interaction the user wants (genuine ideation dialogue), not anything specific to a particular project or subject matter.
